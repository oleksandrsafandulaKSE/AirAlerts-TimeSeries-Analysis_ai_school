## A. Non-air-raid rows: label-variant measurement
- **Variants** (built with the frozen `data.load_official` → `grid.unit_intervals` → `grid.build_grid` →
  `features.build_features` path, same D-020 cap, same gap mask):
  - **L0** = current rule (all levels).
  - **L1** = the same after dropping `level == "hromada"` rows from the shared-format frame BEFORE the union.
    Proxy only: it removes all artillery warnings if the other project's finding holds here, and also some real
    air-raid hromada alerts. No name lists, no fuzzy matching.
- **A1. Descriptive table** `reports/m10/labels_variant.csv`, per unit and per group (Kyiv City / frontline /
  rear; groups from `regions.group_of`, so Kyiv City and Kyivska stay separate), for train
  [2022-03-23, 2026-06-29) and validation [2026-06-29, 2026-07-27), and per calendar quarter (UTC) 2025Q1–2026Q2.
  Definitions (per unit; a group value POOLS its units' numerators and denominators, never averages ratios):
  - onsets = merged-unit onsets (D-023) with start in the period; onsets per 30 days = onsets / (calendar hours
    of the period outside the applied uncertainty zone, `config.uncertainty_zones`) × 720, pooled over units;
  - share of hours under alert = grid hours with `active_at_t == 1` / grid hours outside the uncertainty zone;
  - unmasked rows = eligible rows (mask == 0, label known); base rate = mean y over them;
  - alerts > 24 h = shared-format alerts (by level) with started_at in the period and
    finished_at − started_at > 24 h.
  L0 and L1 side by side, plus the difference.
- **A2. What dropping hromada rows does to onsets**, per unit and quarter. Mutually exclusive classes, using the
  unit's L0 merged intervals (D-020 capped) [s, e) whose start s is an L0 onset:
  - each **L0 onset** s is exactly one of: **kept** (an L1 onset at exactly s); else **shifted** (≥ 1 L1 onset in
    (s, e); shift = the first one − s, median reported); else **disappeared** (no L1 onset in [s, e));
  - each **L1 onset** is exactly one of: **matched** (equals a kept L0 onset); **shift target** (the first L1 onset
    of a shifted L0 interval); else **new in L1** (any other L1 onset — a later start that L0 hid inside one merged
    interval).
  - Invariants asserted: kept + shifted + disappeared = L0 onsets; matched + shift target + new = L1 onsets;
    matched = kept; shift target = shifted.
  Median duration (finished_at − started_at, open ends excluded) of the hromada rows whose started_at equals a
  disappeared L0 onset, and separately of those at a shifted L0 onset — printed, no threshold (their artillery
  rows had median 79 min).
- **A3. Predictability under each variant** (descriptive, not a selection): the M7 V2 recipe trained and scored
  on L0, and separately on L1, on the 4 M7 backtest folds (`FOLD_CUTS` 2025-07-01 … 2026-04-01, fit rows
  t + H ≤ C − 14 d, early stopping [C − 14 d, C) with t + H ≤ C, score [C, C + 8 w)) and on validation.
  Each variant builds its OWN grid, labels, features, recent-rate baseline and offset from its own alerts, with
  the existing causal functions (`improve.features_plus.label_rates`: rate over eligible s ∈ [t − 14 d, t − H],
  causal EXPANDING prior q(t) from labels with s ≤ t − H — used for the OFFSET input only, as in M7 §2.1).
  The REPORTING baseline keeps the D-025 rule unchanged: (k + 10·p_unit) / (n + 10) with p_unit = that variant's
  per-unit rate on the eligible FIT rows of that fold (t + H ≤ C − 14 d; validation run: the train slice), frozen
  during scoring — the `baselines.recent_rate` function called with the variant's grid. Scored on its own
  rows: BSS vs its recent rate, within-unit AUC, PR-AUC and PR-AUC / base rate, base rate, per group. Brier is
  never compared across variants (different targets). Stated in the output header.
- **A4. Hypothesis, pre-declared wording:** "the frontline base rate and the frontline gap to recent rate are
  partly driven by hromada-level rows". The README reports A1–A3 whatever they show, as facts; the
  interpretation stays a hypothesis because L1 is a proxy.

## B. Recency-weighted training
- **Weight** of a fit row at t: `w = 0.5 ** (age_days / h)`, `age_days = (F − t) / 1 day`, where F = the END of
  that fit slice (fold: C − 14 d; validation run: 2026-06-29). Weights only on the fit rows; the early-stopping
  slice is unweighted. LightGBM `Dataset(weight=…)`; nothing else changes.
- **Candidates, fixed in advance:** h ∈ {∞ (= current), 730, 365, 182} days.
- **Base recipe:** M7 V2 (recent-rate offset), the best M7 variant and the live model's recipe.
- **Selection inside train only:** the 4 M7 folds. A candidate passes iff the equal-weighted mean fold Brier drops
  by ≥ 0.5% relative to h = ∞ AND the fold Brier drops in ≥ 3 of 4 folds (the M7 rule). Among passing
  candidates the lowest mean Brier wins; a tie (≤ 1e-6) → the longer half-life. None passes → h = ∞ stays.
  All candidates' fold results are reported.
- **Validation row:** the chosen h (or ∞), trained on train with fit rows t + H ≤ 2026-06-29, early-stopped on
  validation; reported next to V2. Validation is NOT used to choose h.

## C. Hours-under-alert (exposure) features
- **Block D, fixed definitions:** `under_alert_hours_3h`, `_24h`, `_7d` = length, in hours, of the union of the
  unit's D-020-capped intervals (`grid.effective_end`: end = min(finished_at, started_at + 24 h)) intersected
  with (t − w, t], every interval additionally clipped at t. An interval with finished_at > t contributes up to t
  only, so no end after t is read. Same merged-unit intervals as the mask (all levels; L0 only).
- **Gap rule (D-023):** the APPLIED zone is `config.uncertainty_zones(CFG)` = [2026-08-29 00:00, 2026-09-04 00:00)
  UTC (approved gap + 24 h post-gap mask; the detected edges 05:00 → 13:00 are only a check, D-022). Value is NaN
  iff (t − w, t] ∩ [z0, z1) ≠ ∅, i.e. t ≥ z0 and t − w < z1 (the existing `_zone_nan` predicate). D-024 drops
  the row (counted). So `_7d` recovers at t = 2026-09-11 00:00 (after the test end), `_24h` at 2026-09-05 00:00,
  `_3h` at 2026-09-04 03:00.
- **Acceptance:** greedy, after §B (reference = V2 with the chosen h). Same M7 rule on the same 4 folds, scored
  on identical keys (rows where every Block D value is known). Reported accepted or not.
- **Leakage (tests/test_improve_leakage.py, reusing `truncation_check`):**
  - causal block D unchanged by truncation at pinned real witnesses: an alert active at t; an alert ending
    exactly at t; an alert starting in (t, t + 1h]; a standing alert past 24 h; the gap edges; a DST day;
  - **negative control:** the same feature computed WITHOUT clipping at t (full capped duration of every
    interval that starts in the window) must be caught at an exact, non-empty, pinned witness set.
- Known limitation (M9 A5): an end written later by the parser's 1-hour imputation is not visible to the
  truncation test; stated in the docstring.

## D. Stability tables (validation; test only via §E)
- **Inputs:** saved predictions only. Validation: `reports/official/val_predictions.csv` (`p_headline`) joined
  to `reports/improve/validation_predictions.csv` (`recent_rate`, `V2`) on (unit, t), UTC-aware, unique keys,
  identical key sets, identical `y` to the bit (as M9 B3); any mismatch raises. Plus the §B/§C chosen variant's
  validation predictions, saved by this milestone.
- **Slices:** (a) week = Monday 00:00 UTC weeks, partial weeks kept with their real rows; (b) Kyiv day 06:00–21:59
  vs night 22:00–05:59 local, from `tz_convert("Europe/Kyiv")` of t. Groups from `regions.group_of`.
- **Per slice × group × predictor:** rows, positives, base rate, Brier, BSS vs recent rate (pooled ratio
  1 − ΣB_model / ΣB_rr), pooled AUC, mean within-unit AUC (only units with ≥ 10 positives AND ≥ 10 negatives in
  that slice, D-025; excluded units listed; NaN with a reason when none qualifies), PR-AUC, PR-AUC / base rate,
  low-n flag (as `evaluate.py`). Zero denominators → NaN with a printed reason (D-025).
- Output `reports/m10/stability_validation.csv` (consumed by M11).

## Testing
- `pytest -q` full suite, including both fingerprint tests and every existing leakage test.
- New tests:
  - `labels_variant`: L1 drops exactly the hromada rows; L0 output equals the frozen pipeline's output on real
    data (onset count pinned). A2 fixtures, one unit each: (i) hromada [00:00, 01:00) alone → 1 disappeared;
    (ii) hromada [00:00, 02:00) + raion [01:00, 03:00) → 1 shifted (60 min), 1 shift target, 0 new;
    (iii) hromada [00:00, 05:00) + raion [01:00, 02:00) + raion [03:00, 04:00) → 1 shifted, 1 shift target
    (01:00), 1 new (03:00); (iv) raion [00:00, 01:00) + hromada [00:30, 02:00) → 1 kept, 1 matched; all
    invariants hold.
  - reporting baseline: the variant's p_unit comes from fold-fit rows only (mutating labels after C − 14 d leaves
    it unchanged).
  - **variant isolation:** mutating L1 labels at s > t − H leaves L1 recent rate and offset at t unchanged;
    mutating them at s = t − H changes them (the window's right end); fold keys keep t + H = C and drop
    t + H > C; an onset exactly at t is not in y(t), one at t + H is.
  - `recency`: weights equal 1 at age 0, 0.5 at age h, 0.25 at 2h; the EXACT finite weight vector reaches the
    fit `lgb.Dataset` (captured via `get_weight()`); h = ∞ reproduces V2's validation predictions to 1e-12; the
    early-stopping Dataset has `get_weight() is None`.
  - `exposure`: hand-calculated fixture (two overlapping intervals; one carried in from before the window; one
    running past t with an unknown end; one > 24 h crossing the window start) → exact hours; pinned values at
    t = z0 − 1 h (numeric), t = z0 (NaN), t = z1 + w − 1 h (NaN) and t = z1 + w (numeric) for each w;
    truncation + negative control as in §C.
  - `stability`: unequal synthetic predictions with a hand-calculated BSS (e.g. B_model = 0.08, B_rr = 0.10 →
    0.2, sign and denominator checked); model = recent rate → 0; Kyiv 05:00/06:00 and 21:00/22:00 transitions
    assigned night/day/day/night; the repeated hour on 2025-10-26 and the skipped hour on 2026-03-29 assign by
    local time; Kyiv City and Kyivska with different values stay separate; the join raises on a key or y
    mismatch; a slice with no qualifying unit gives NaN within-unit AUC with a reason.
  - `run_m10 --posthoc-test` raises without the approval file.
- Print shapes, date ranges and base rates at each stage (CLAUDE.md).
